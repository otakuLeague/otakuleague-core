# OTA-155: auth and onboarding mockups

- Changed: auth and onboarding mockups in `docs/design/auth/` (sign in, sign up, reset, check email, verified/expired, sign-in prompt dialog, `/onboarding`), with 12 screenshots in `shots/` and `motion.md`. Design only, no app code.
- Hard email verification for email sign-ups: no session until the link is used; "Check your email" is session-less and identical for new and existing addresses.
- No account enumeration: one sign-in error for unknown, wrong-password and Google-only accounts; forgot password always gives the same answer; username reserved 24 h after any sign-up submit; resend link on every failed sign-in.
- Tokens are exchanged on first hit, then 303 to a clean URL with `Referrer-Policy: no-referrer`; the check-email address comes from a short-lived flash cookie, not `?email=`.
- `next` is same-origin only; the sign-in prompt stores the pending add in sessionStorage and the anime page completes it after sign-in.
- Onboarding trimmed to 3 steps (username if generated, avatar crop, bio), skippable via a POST; birthday and banner moved out (banner not built in v1 unless email change is scoped).
- Next: OTA-138 auth spike + ADR must record the token-in-URL exception and confirm link lifetimes (24 h confirm, 1 h reset, single-use); then OTA-139, OTA-140.
