About us

Anime tracking that feels like a game.

Five quick answers: what OtakuLeague is, where it's going, and how to reach the person building it.

“My watchlist lived in four apps and none of them made it fun. OtakuLeague is my fix: one place to track, rank and level up. It's a one-person project built in the open, so if something's broken or missing, tell me.”

OL
The founderBuilding OtakuLeague solo
01What is OtakuLeague?

OtakuLeague is one home for everything you watch: track your anime, find the next one, and earn XP for it. Your list, your stats and a seasonal leaderboard live in the same place, so keeping up with a show feels like progress, not admin.

02What can I expect?

Today you get anime pages with what you need to decide, seasonal charts and rankings, a list that keeps up with you, and levels with a leaderboard that resets every season.

Next: quizzes, games and more ways to play with other fans. The public roadmap ↗ shows what I'm building and lets you vote on what comes first.

03How does it work?
  • Browse freely, no account needed.
  • Sign up (free) and add anime to your list as you watch.
  • Progress earns XP. XP raises your level for good, and the XP you earn during a season ranks you on that season's leaderboard.
04How do I report a bug?

Tell me what you did, what you expected and what happened (a screenshot helps) through the contact form, or post it on our Discord ↗. I read every report.

05Is it free?

Yes. Everything you see today is free. Later I may add optional extras to help pay for the servers, but the plan is for tracking, XP and the leaderboard to stay free.
