The short version

  • We collect what your account needs to work: email, username, your list and your XP. Nothing to sell, no ads.
  • Only essential cookies: one keeps you signed in, a few short ones carry sign-in steps. No cookie banner because there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Our server and database run in an EU region, and account emails go through Brevo, whose servers are in France, Germany and Belgium.
  • Delete your account any time in Settings: it disappears from the site at once, and from our backups when they roll over.

Only Brevo's locations (France, Germany, Belgium) are confirmed. Vercel runs our functions in an EU region (ADR 0009); confirm Railway's and Sentry's regions and the backup window in OTA-147 before stating more.

Who we are#

OtakuLeague (otakuleague.com) is an independent project run by one person, its founder, who is responsible for your data (the “controller”). Write to contact@otakuleague.com about anything in this policy.

Build: add the controller's legal name and postal address once Farouk confirms them (GDPR art. 13).

What we collect#

When you create an account

  • Email address: to sign in and to send account emails (confirmation, password reset, email change).
  • Username: public, it is your profile address.
  • Password: stored only as an argon2id hash. We never see or store the password itself.
  • If you use Google: your Google account's email and a Google user ID.

When you use the site

  • Your profile, if you add them: photo, banner, bio, birthday (the year is never shown), profile visibility.
  • Your list: anime, status, episodes, scores, favourites, and the XP they earn.
  • Preferences: adult content and language.
  • Contact messages: what you write in the contact form, with the email you give.

Automatically

To stop abuse (too many sign-in attempts, spam), we count attempts under keyed hashes of your IP address and of the email or username you sign in with, never the values themselves, and delete them within about 48 hours. When something crashes, an error report records what broke and the page, without your password or messages.

Why we use it#

To run your account and the features you use (contract), to keep the service safe and working (legitimate interest), and to answer you when you write to us (legitimate interest). We do not sell your data, show ads, or build advertising profiles. Account emails contain no tracking pixels and no tracked links.

Launch gate, not yet verified: Brevo open and click tracking must be off (ADR 0015, OTA-178). Keep this sentence only once that is checked.

How long we keep it#

  • Your account: until you delete it. Deleting removes your profile, list, XP and sign-ins from the site at once; database backups drop it when they roll over.
  • Sign-ups never confirmed: deleted within about 48 hours (they expire after 24 hours and a daily cleanup removes them).
  • Keyed hashes of IPs and sign-in identifiers: within about 48 hours.
  • Contact messages: as long as the conversation needs, then deleted.
  • Error reports: as long as our Sentry plan keeps them.

Build: confirm the backup rotation window (how long a deleted account can survive in a database backup) and Sentry's retention.

Your rights#

You can see and fix most of your data yourself in Settings and on your profile, and delete everything with Delete account. You can also ask us, by email, for a copy of your data, to correct or erase it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. We answer within one month. If you think we got something wrong, you can complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Security#

Everything travels over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with argon2id. The session cookie is HttpOnly, Secure and locked to our domain. Changing your password signs out every other device.

Children#

OtakuLeague is not meant for children under 15. Adult content stays off unless your birthday shows you are 18 or older.

Minimum age (13, 15 or 16) to confirm with the Terms in OTA-147.

Changes#

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we update the date at the top and tell signed-in users on the site before it applies.

Contact#

Questions or requests: contact@otakuleague.com or the contact form.
