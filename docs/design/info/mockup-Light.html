OtakuLeague doesn't write anime information by hand. Titles, episodes, studios, characters and more come from the communities and services below. We cache their data to keep pages fast, show where it comes from, and follow each one's terms.

AniList#

Most anime facts (titles, synopses, episodes, studios, characters, airing schedules) come from the AniList API, and covers and banners are served from AniList's image servers. Data © AniList and its contributors.

MyAnimeList#

Scores and member counts shown as “MAL” come from MyAnimeList. MyAnimeList is a trademark of its owner; OtakuLeague is not affiliated with it. Importing a MyAnimeList XML export only reads the file you upload.

AnimeThemes#

Opening and ending themes come from AnimeThemes. Videos play from their servers; we don't host or copy them.

Images and artwork#

Anime artwork belongs to its studios and publishers. We show it to identify the anime, link to the source, and remove any image on request.

Report a problem#

Wrong data, or something you own shown here? Tell us and we fix or remove it.

MyAnimeList and AnimeThemes are listed only if their use is approved (ADR 0006 is still proposed, pending permission answers); the footer credit follows the same rule. Confirm each source's licence and attribution wording before caching (OTA-147).
