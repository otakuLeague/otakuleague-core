Synopsis

Humanity has lived behind three giant walls for a century, safe from the Titans that roam outside and eat people.

When a colossal Titan breaks through the outer wall, young Eren Yeager watches his town fall and swears to wipe every Titan from the earth.

He joins the military with his friends Mikasa and Armin, and starts to uncover what the Titans really are.
